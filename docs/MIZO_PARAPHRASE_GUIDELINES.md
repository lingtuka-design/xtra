# MIZO TRANSLATION & PARAPHRASE GUIDELINES & 52 GOLD-STANDARD EXAMPLES

## 1. Mizo Grammar & Linguistic Rules

1. **Sentence Structure (SOV - Subject, Object, Verb)**:
   Mizo ṭawng ziah dan phung pangngai (K. Zawla grammar) - Subject hmasak ber, Object a dawt, a tawpah Verb (e.g., "Sakeiin sial a seh", "Arsenal chuan player an la lut").

2. **Adjective Placement**:
   Adjective chu noun hnungah zel a awm tur a ni (e.g., "sebawng dum", "player thar", "inkhel hmuhnawm").

3. **Subject Markers (-n / -a'n / -in)**:
   - Mihring hmingah: `-n` / `-a'n` (e.g., Fabrizio Romano-a'n, Amorim-a'n, Carrick-a'n, Arteta-a'n, Rashford-a'n).
   - Team / Club / Pawl / Rannungah: `-in` (e.g., Manchester United-in, Chelsea-in, Real Madrid-in, Arsenal-in, FIFA-in, UEFA-in).

4. **Foreign Words leh Hyphen (-)**:
   Hnam dang hming leh hmun hming zawmah hyphen hman ngei ngei tur (e.g., Old Trafford-ah, San Siro-ah, Stamford Bridge-ah, Pep Guardiola-a hnuaiah).

5. **Thumal Khap Bur (Vocabulary Bans)**:
   - **"rasmi" hman khap tlat a ni**: "Official" sawi nan "rasmi" tih hman phal a ni lo. Official, an nemnghet, thuchhuah an siam, puan a ni tih hman tur.
   - **Ṭawng thar phuah chawp khap a ni**: Football ṭawngkam pangngai (contract, deal, medical, loan, transfer, assist, counter-attack, winger, midfielder, striker, clean sheet) te chu a nihna angin hman mai tur.

6. **'lak' leh 'la' Hman Dan Dik**:
   - Noun / Tumna sawi nan: "lak" (e.g., player lak luh tumna, lak an duh).
   - Verb / Tih tawh hnu sawi nan: "la" (e.g., player thar an la tawh a, free-in an la bawk a, an la lut fel ta; "an lak tawh a" tih loh tur).

7. **Khelmual Sawi Dan**:
   - Home -> mahni mual / mualneitu
   - Away -> khualmual / khualmualah
   - Two-legged tie -> home and away-a inhmachhawnna

8. **Hun Sawi Dan (Time Expressions)**:
   - "this week" -> tun kar
   - "this year" -> kumin
   - "this month" -> tun thla
   - "this season" / "this term" -> tun season ("he season" tih loh tur)
   - "last season" / "last year" -> season liam ta (European season August aṭanga May milin)
   - "today" -> vawiin

9. **Mizo ṭawng tluang pangngai**:
   Word-for-word a letling bur lovin, Mizo ṭawng kalphung dik, chhiar hahdam leh nuam tak turin paraphrase/translate ṭhin tur a ni. A point-ah luh chat chat tur.

10. **Bracket hman loh**:
    Mizo ṭawnga letlin fiah tawk sa rengah bracket hmanga English thumal dah tel kher loh tur a ni.
