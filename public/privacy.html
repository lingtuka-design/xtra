Privacy Policy for Xtra

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Xtra ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website and automated football news aggregation service.

Information We Collect

Xtra does not collect, store, or sell any personal identifying information from visitors. We aggregate public football news updates from verified public social media sources and translate them into Mizo for our community.

Facebook & Meta Platform Data

Our application uses Meta Graph API only to publish authorized football news updates to the designated Facebook Page (Inkhel Xtra) managed by our administrators. We do not access, store, or share any personal user data from Facebook users or followers.
